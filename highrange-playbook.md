# High-Range Playbook — Executable (Top 3 Paths)

For Ruth. Tone: encouraging, honest, no hype. Estimates marked (est.). Currency USD unless noted.
Pick ONE path. Run 90 days before judging. Charge from day 1. Track hours/revenue/hourly rate; kill below $20/hr after 30 days.

---

## Path A — Productized Service ($500–$2,000/client)

One fixed-scope, fixed-price service. Systematize delivery; raise price as demand grows.

### First 7 days (quickstart)
- Day 1: Write the one-sentence offer: "I will [outcome] in [time] for [price]."
- Day 2: List 10 people who have the problem. DM 5 (value first, no pitch).
- Day 3: Do 1 free/cheap case study for a friend or local business.
- Day 4: Write scope doc + agreement template.
- Day 5: Set price at $500 (or $250 first 2 clients for case studies).
- Day 6: Post offer in 3 communities + LinkedIn/X.
- Day 7: Follow up with everyone who replied; book 1 call.

### 90-day plan
- Wk 1–2: Niche + offer + case study #1. Goal: 1 paid or free case study.
- Wk 3–4: Package delivery checklist (repeatable). Land 2 paying clients at $500.
- Wk 5–6: Raise to $750. Add 1 testimonial (real, earned). Referral ask.
- Wk 7–8: Systematize: templates, SOP, intake form. Aim 4 clients total.
- Wk 9–10: Raise to $1,000. Add case study #2. Start content (1 post/wk).
- Wk 11–12: Review metrics. Decide: scale (hire VA) or niche down. Target: $3k/mo.

### Outreach email template
Subject: Quick idea for [their company/project]
Hi [Name],
I noticed [specific, genuine observation]. I help [niche] get [outcome] in [timeframe] — fixed price, no scope creep.
Would a 15-min chat be useful? If not, no worries — here's a free [tip/resource] either way.
— Ruth

### Sales page outline
1. Headline: outcome + timeframe ("Hermes agent setup in 5 days — fixed price")
2. Who it's for (2-3 bullets)
3. What's included (checklist)
4. What's NOT included (scope guard)
5. Price + what happens next
6. FAQ (3-5 real objections)
7. Testimonial (only real ones)
8. CTA button

### Pricing ladder
- Client 1-2: $250 (case-study rate, in exchange for testimonial + permission)
- Client 3-5: $500
- Client 6-10: $750
- 10+: $1,000+ (raise until demand drops 20%)

### Tools (free/cheap)
- Cal.com (free) for booking; Stripe/PayPal for invoicing; Notion or Google Docs for scope; Loom (free) for async demos; Gmail.

### Metrics
- Outreach sent, reply rate, calls booked, close rate, revenue, hours/client, effective $/hr, referrals.

### Kill criteria
- < $20/hr effective after 30 days → niche down or switch.
- 0 replies after 50 personalized outreaches → offer unclear.
- Scope creep >20% of projects → tighten scope doc.

### Pitfalls
- Vague scope → endless revisions. Fix with written agreement.
- Underpricing forever. Raise on schedule.
- Doing custom work for productized price. Say no or re-quote.

## Path B — Premium Product ($99–$499/sale)

One comprehensive template/system/course. Low marginal effort after build; high per-sale.

### First 7 days (quickstart)
- Day 1: Pick a painful, specific niche (not "productivity" → "Notion CRM for freelancers").
- Day 2: Survey 5 people in the niche: what's the #1 pain?
- Day 3: Outline the product (modules/sections). Aim 1-3 hours of value.
- Day 4: Build v1 (ugly but complete). No polish.
- Day 5: Create cover image + 30s demo video (screen record).
- Day 6: Set up Gumroad listing at $99. Write sales copy.
- Day 7: Share in 3 communities (value-first post + link at end). Ask 2 friends to review.

### 90-day plan
- Wk 1–2: Niche + v1 build + listing live. Goal: first sale (any price).
- Wk 3–4: Collect 2 real testimonials. Add 1 bonus. Price test $99 vs $149.
- Wk 5–6: Content engine: 1 tutorial/wk showing the product in use.
- Wk 7–8: Affiliate outreach (3 creators in niche). Add FAQ from real objections.
- Wk 9–10: Bundle/upsell ($199 tier with bonus). Email list start (free lead magnet).
- Wk 11–12: Launch v2 with improvements. Target: $1k/mo or 10 sales/mo.

### Sales page outline
1. Headline: outcome ("Ship your first paid template in 7 days")
2. Problem (agitate)
3. Solution (what's inside, with screenshots)
4. Who it's for / not for
5. Bonuses
6. Price + guarantee (refund if not useful)
7. Testimonials (real)
8. FAQ
9. CTA

### Pricing ladder
- Launch: $49 (early-bird, first 10 buyers)
- Standard: $99
- Premium bundle: $199 (product + 1hr consult or extra templates)
- Never discount below $49; raise instead.

### Tools (free/cheap)
- Gumroad (free tier, ~10% fee) or Lemon Squeezy; Canva (free) for cover; OBS/screen record; Notion/Markdown for content.

### Metrics
- Views, conversion rate, sales, refunds, revenue, traffic source, email signups.

### Kill criteria
- < 1 sale per 200 views after 60 days → niche or offer problem.
- Refund rate > 10% → product doesn't deliver.
- 0 organic traffic after 90 days → need content/SEO investment or switch.

### Pitfalls
- Building for 3 weeks before showing anyone. Ship in 7 days.
- Generic niche → no one buys. Be specific.
- Fake testimonials → Honor -20. Never.

## Path C — Micro-SaaS (MRR, $9–$29/mo)

Small paid tool with subscription. High effort upfront, recurring revenue.

### First 7 days (quickstart)
- Day 1: Find a recurring pain (something people do weekly). Check 3 communities for complaints.
- Day 2: Validate: "Would you pay $9/mo for X?" — need 5 yes.
- Day 3: Define MVP scope: ONE feature, ONE user type. Write spec (1 page).
- Day 4: Pick stack: FastAPI + SQLite + simple HTML (no framework needed). Host on free tier.
- Day 5: Build auth + core feature (ugly UI ok).
- Day 6: Add billing (Stripe test mode) + cancel flow.
- Day 7: Deploy (Render/Railway free tier or VPS). Invite 3 beta users free for feedback.

### 90-day plan
- Wk 1–2: MVP + beta (3-5 users). Fix bugs. Goal: 1 paying user.
- Wk 3–4: Charge from day 1 of "launch" ($9/mo). Launch on Product Hunt / HN / communities.
- Wk 5–6: Onboarding email sequence. Track churn reasons (ask canceling users).
- Wk 7–8: Add 1 most-requested feature. Improve activation (time-to-value < 5 min).
- Wk 9–10: Content: 1 case study + 1 tutorial. SEO landing page.
- Wk 11–12: Review MRR + churn. Target: $300 MRR or 30 users. Decide scale/pivot.

### Pricing ladder
- $9/mo (solo) / $19/mo (pro) / $49/mo (team) — keep 2 tiers max at start.
- Annual: 2 months free. Easy cancel always.

### Tools (free/cheap)
- Python/FastAPI, SQLite/Postgres (Supabase free), Stripe, Render/Railway free tier, GitHub Actions CI, Plausible (optional).

### Metrics
- Signups, activation rate, MRR, churn rate, LTV, CAC (≈0 if organic), support tickets.

### Kill criteria
- < 5 paying users after 60 days → pivot niche or kill.
- Churn > 10%/mo after 90 days → product not sticky.
- Support burden > 5 hrs/wk at < $300 MRR → unsustainable.

### Pitfalls
- Building features nobody asked for. Ship MVP in 2 weeks.
- No cancel flow / dark patterns → chargebacks. Easy cancel.
- Ignoring churn. Ask every canceler why.
- Over-engineering. SQLite + simple HTML is fine for v1.

## Universal rules (all paths)
- Pick ONE. Run 90 days before judging.
- Charge from day 1 (free users don't validate).
- Track: hours, revenue, $/hr. Kill below $20/hr after 30 days.
- Never buy fake reviews, never plagiarize, never promise undeliverable results.
- Taxes: track income from day one.

## Red flags (avoid)
"Passive income" courses, crypto pumps, MLM, dropshipping with no differentiation, AI-spam content farms, bought engagement.
