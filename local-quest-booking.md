
### Before you leave home
- [ ] Confirm address + time by text the night before.
- [ ] Charge phone, bring charger cable, pen, small notebook.
- [ ] Bring water; plan route (transit time + 10 min buffer).

### On arrival (first 2 minutes)
- [ ] Greet, smile, introduce yourself.
- [ ] Confirm scope + price OUT LOUD: "So today: [X], for R$[Y], about [Z] minutes. Sound right?"
- [ ] Get Wi-Fi password if needed; ask where they want you to work.

### During the job
- [ ] Work only on what was quoted. If you find extra work, STOP and say: "I can add [X] for R$[Y] — want me to?"
- [ ] Explain each step in plain words; let them watch if they want.
- [ ] Take a photo of the final result (for your portfolio + proof of delivery).
- [ ] If you hit something outside your skill, stop: "This needs someone more skilled — I can finish [X] today and refer you for [Y]."

### Closing (last 3 minutes)
- [ ] Show them what changed + teach 1 thing they can do themselves.
- [ ] Confirm they're happy: "Does everything look right?"
- [ ] Request payment: "Pix is easiest — I'll send the key. Cash also fine."
- [ ] Send Pix key; wait for confirmation before leaving.
- [ ] Ask for referral (script below).

### After you leave (within 1 hour)
- [ ] Send a thank-you text + the photo of the result.
- [ ] Ask for referral (if not already asked).
- [ ] Log in your tracker: date, client, service, price, time spent, rating (1-5), notes.
- [ ] Update availability for next slots.

## 4. Always-get-paid rules
1. **Quote before you start.** No "we'll figure it out later."
2. **Pix before you leave.** Don't leave unpaid hoping they'll pay later.
3. **No free "quick looks."** A 5-min diagnostic is free; the fix is paid.
4. **Scope in writing (text).** "Today: X for R$Y" — screenshot it.
5. **No-show policy:** if client isn't home after 15 min, charge a R$10 trip fee or reschedule once.
6. **Redo policy:** if something you did breaks within 48h, fix it free. If it's a new problem, quote again.
7. **Never work without a clear price.** "Let me check and get back to you with a price" is fine; "I'll just do it and we'll settle later" is not.

| "Can you come back tomorrow?" | "Yes — I'll note it. Same price, and I'll confirm the time tonight." |
