## Honest noob framing tips
- Say "early-career" and "learning by shipping" — true and attractive to teams that value growth.
- List real tools you've actually used, even lightly. Don't claim seniority.
- Show process (writeups, commits, demos) instead of credentials.
- Never invent testimonials, clients, or metrics. If asked, say "in progress."
